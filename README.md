# Nourish-Your-Soul-Healthy-Habits-App
This app is designed to help people set encouraging goals that support physical and mental health. It has highly customizable settings to suite users' specific needs. Users create goals, set the specs, log progress and log mood and energy states throughout the day. Users can view their progress and statistics so they can learn about their habits.

NOURISH YOUR SOUL
Healthy Habits App

Nourish Your Soul is a goal tracking app with an emphasis on mental health. The app is designed to be supportive to people who are dealing with, or have dealt with,  mental health disorders, as well as people who generally want to maintain mental and physical well-being. The app is based around goals that users can set to their own specifications. Once a goal is set, users can log progress, add notes, edit the goal and view their progress history and habits statistics. The home screen is the 'Daily Log' where users enter their progress for the day. There are also 'Calendar', 'Stats', 'Goals' and 'Settings tabs'. 

The Welcome Pop Up Summary
Every time a user opens the app, the welcome pop up opens with an optional motivational message, and their current goal progress.

DAILY LOG
This is the home page. This is where users log progress for each of their goals. They can also add reflections and notes to their progress for specific goals. Below the progress log sections, there is a mood and energy tracker where users give their "pleasantness" and "energy" a number from one to ten. They can add hash tags to describe their mood and add any notes. Below the mood and energy spectrum, if turned on in settings, there is a cycle tracker. It shows the mood cycle for the day, and users can log their flow and any symptoms they are experiencing that day.

CALENDAR
In the calendar, users can view a weekly or monthly summary of their progress denoted with stars. Daily goals show up as a dot on each day, while weekly and monthly goals show up as a line spanning the duration of the goal. When a user logs progress, a star shows up on the day in the color they chose for that goal. If they complete a goal, the star for the day gains a gold outline. If cycle symptoms have been logged, the flow will show up in the calendar as a darker or lighter line depending on their flow for the day so that the full duration while the user is bleeding will show a continuous red, or blue, line. Users can click on specific days to view their summary for that day.

STATS
This is where users can view all of their progress statistics. They can view their goal progress as a line or bar graph. They are able to view all of their goals at one, or view specific goals on the graph. Other states, such as their average daily goal progress, are also available. Below the graphs, there is a bubble graph of all mood and energy hashtags. Below that, there is a bubble graph for all logged cycle symptoms.

GOALS
In the goals sections, users can set new goals or edit their current goals. Within the 'Add Goal' function, users name their goals and choose a domain from the 9 options. The options are: Nutrition, Movement, Nature, Human Connection, Learning, Medication/Supplements, Hydration, Sleep and Miscellaneous. Then, users set specifications for each goal including frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), goal type, goal units, goal schedule and goal color. Within the settings for each goal, users can elect to track the goal as a habit only whereby they do not set a target goal but can still log progress each day. For example, if a user wanted to log how many hours they slept each night, but did not want to work towards a specific target like 8 hours, they could choose to track their sleep as a habit only. Users can also choose to limit logging progress to the set goal. For example, if a user wanted to sleep at least 8 hours each night, but beyond that they did not care how much more they slept, they could choose 'stop logging progress once goal is complete' at which point the button to add progress for that goal becomes non-functional. Users have the option to set challenge goals on top of their goal which is designed to encourage users to set highly achievable goals while celebrating reaching progress beyond that. For example, a user could set a sleep goal of 8 hours per night with a challenge goal of 9 hours per night. 

>Medication/supplement Goals-This goal has specific options within it that are not applicable to the other domains
>Within medication/supplement goals, users can add all of the medications and supplements they take including their dosage and how frequently they take it. Within that, they can specify if they only take the medication 'as needed' in which case "progress" is logged similarly to a habit. There is no target goal for 'as needed' medications but users can still track whenever they take them.'Stop logging progress once goal is complete' is automatically on for ALL medication/supplement goals and cannot be turned off. This is to encourage users to only take what has been prescribed to them. If the user has any medications that they only take 'as needed,' they can still log progress for those medications.
>Nutrition Goals-Nutrition goals are designed to celebrate eating and taking care of our bodies. Users are encouraged to eat intuitively and nourish their soul with the food that makes their body, and mind, the happiest. Users cannot count calories in Nourish Your Soul.

SETTINGS
This is where users can set up the app to work for them. Users can set the 'week start day' and 'day cut off time' specific to their schedule. They can also customize their daily log view, welcome pop up options, motivational messages, and congratulatory pop up options. Settings is also where users can enable the cycle tracker or not. There is an 'appearance theme' option where users choose between 'light pastel' and 'dark mode.' If the user want to clear their data, they can do so, and, they can also export their data at any point.
